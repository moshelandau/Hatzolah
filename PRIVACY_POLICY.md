# Privacy Policy - Hatzolah Members App

**Last updated: April 7, 2026**

## Overview

The Hatzolah Members App ("App") is designed exclusively for registered Hatzolah volunteer members. This privacy policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your data.

## Data Collection

### SMS Data
- The App monitors incoming SMS messages **only** from your organization's configured dispatch number.
- Dispatch SMS messages are parsed locally on your device to extract emergency addresses.
- No SMS content is transmitted to external servers.

### Location Data
- GPS location is tracked **only during active emergency calls** to calculate mileage.
- Location data is stored locally on your device and tied to your personal call records.
- Location tracking stops automatically when a call is completed.
- Your location history is visible only to you.

### Patient Information
- Patient names, dates of birth, and medical notes entered during call documentation are stored locally on your device.
- Patient data is **never shared** with other app users.
- Patient documentation is accessible only to the individual member who created it.

### Member Information
- Member names, phone numbers, WhatsApp contacts, and email addresses are stored in the app database.
- This directory information is visible to all authenticated app members.

## Data Storage

- All data is stored locally on your device using encrypted Android Room database.
- No patient data, call details, or personal analytics are stored on external servers.

## Data Sharing

- **We do not sell, trade, or share personal data with third parties.**
- Patient information is never shared between app users.
- Personal call history and analytics are private to each individual user.
- Only aggregate team statistics (total calls, total miles, average response time) are visible to all members, with no individual breakdowns.

## HIPAA Considerations

This App is designed with HIPAA-conscious principles:
- Patient data is isolated per user and never shared across accounts.
- No patient health information (PHI) is transmitted over the network.
- Individual call details and patient notes are private to the documenting member.
- The App does not create, maintain, or transmit electronic health records.

## Permissions Used

| Permission | Purpose |
|---|---|
| SMS (Receive/Read/Send) | Monitor dispatch messages, send verification codes |
| Location (Fine/Background) | Track mileage during active emergency calls |
| Phone (Call) | Quick-dial RMA hotline |
| Notifications | Alert members of incoming dispatch calls on lock screen |
| Internet | Future sync capabilities |

## Data Retention

- Call logs and documentation are retained on the device until manually deleted by the user.
- Uninstalling the App removes all locally stored data.

## Security

- Access requires SMS verification against the member database.
- Only pre-registered phone numbers (added by an admin) can access the App.
- All data remains on the local device.

## Contact

For questions about this privacy policy or data practices, contact your Hatzolah chapter administrator.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Users will be notified of significant changes through the App.
